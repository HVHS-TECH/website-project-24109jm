Imagine Dragons

Imagine Dragons are a world renowned band famous for their incredible number of hits.

That really is no exageration. In February, 2024, they became the first band in the world to hit 10 songs with over 1 Billion streams on Spotify. Currently, they hold 11 songs at that milestone, just one away from the top (12) that Coldplay and Maroon 5 share. They also have 1 song (Believer) that has over 3 Billion streams, and they have 2 songs (Demons and Thunder) that are very close. When those 2 songs hit, they will be tied for the band with the most songs at 3 Billion streams. There is a fan-led project called The Natural Project, where we are trying to push Natural all the up to 3 Billion Streams as well to make it the clear first place.

Here are the 11 songs with 1B+ streams ordered from most to popular to least on Spotify (as of 30/03/2026):

  1. Believer (3.79B)
  2. Demons (2.98B)
  3. Thunder (2.98B)
  4. Radioactive (2.29B)
  5. Enemy (with JID) (1.97B)
  6. Bones (1.76B)
  7. Whatever It Takes (1.67B)
  8. Natural (1.55B)
  9. Bad Liar (1.37B)
  10. Sucker For Pain (with 5 other artists) (1.32B)
  11. On Top Of The World (1.22B)

Some firebreathers love them for their simple lyrics that's usually easy and instant to relate to. They weren't always like that though. Back in the Night Visions and Smoke + Mirrors eras, their lyrics was more complicated. Fans are devided on what is better. Personally I prefer their newer style as I can connect to it much faster, and therefore stronger. Others like complexity and metaphors because it brings more depth. There is no right or wrong answer. And regardless which you prefer, every single one, regardless which it is, is full stop incredible.
